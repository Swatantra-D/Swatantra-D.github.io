This project, **“Spatio-temporal characterization of network dynamics across healthy ageing and diseases”**, looks at how patterns of brain activity unfold in space and time, and how those patterns relate to the structure of the brain's networks as people age.

It compares healthy ageing across tasks with clinical conditions such as mild cognitive impairment (MCI) and Alzheimer's disease.
