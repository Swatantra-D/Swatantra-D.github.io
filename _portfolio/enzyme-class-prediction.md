Enzymes are grouped into classes according to the reaction they catalyse. In **“Enzyme Class prediction from peptide statistics data with Artificial Neural Networks”**, I classified enzymes from their chemical features, statistics computed from their peptide sequences, using artificial neural networks and random forests.
