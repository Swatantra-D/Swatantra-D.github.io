During sleep, the hippocampus produces brief, fast bursts of activity called sharp-wave ripples, which are thought to help consolidate memories. The project **“Analyzing the effect of cannabidiol and anesthesia on sleep and memory formation”** needed these events to be found and measured reliably across long recordings.

My part was on the analysis side: automatic detection and assessment of ripples and of non-REM sleep.
