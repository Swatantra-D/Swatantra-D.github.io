At any moment, whole-brain activity tends to settle into one of a handful of recurring patterns, or *states*, before moving on to another. My MS thesis, **“Characterizing Age-Associated Slowing Down of Speed of Transitions in Brain States”**, characterises how the speed of these transitions changes with age.

The work uses fMRI recorded while people watched a movie, rested, and performed a sensorimotor task, so that the same question can be asked across very different conditions. A poster on this work, “Age-associated slowing down of speed of transitions during naturalistic movie watching”, was presented at ACCS12, BraiNDy 2026.
