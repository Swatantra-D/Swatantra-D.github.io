Optical coherence tomography (OCT) produces cross-sectional images of tissue using light, much as ultrasound does with sound. My master's thesis, **“Deep Learning Applications on Cutaneous Wound Healing From Optical Coherence Tomography Scans of Small Animals”**, applied deep-learning models to OCT scans of skin from small animals, with the goal of segmenting skin layers to characterise wounds.

A related paper, “Deep Learning based Skin-layer Segmentation for Characterizing Cutaneous Wounds from Optical Coherence Tomography Images”, appeared at the 45th Annual International Conference of the IEEE Engineering in Medicine and Biology Society (EMBC 2023).
